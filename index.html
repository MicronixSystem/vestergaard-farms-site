How we farm

Nothing here is a marketing claim.

Every one of these is something you could check by walking the fields with us.

Our practices in full
The herd on open pasture

Rotational grazing

The herd moves to fresh grass regularly, so the pasture recovers, the soil builds and the animals eat what they were built to eat.

112 acres in rotation

Cow and calf on clean ground

Chemical-free fields

No synthetic herbicides, pesticides, fungicides or fertilisers touch this ground, and no GMO grain goes into any animal we raise.

Since the first season

Hand feeding at the fence

Low-stress handling

Our yards and our routine follow Temple Grandin's methods, because a calm animal is both the humane outcome and the better one.

Grandin-based practice
