Grass fed and grass finished

The second word is the one that matters.

Almost every beef animal in America starts on grass. The label "grass fed" only tells you how it began. What changes the meat is the last four months, and most cattle spend those months on grain in a feedlot. Ours never leave the pasture.

Send us a question
Is your beef grass finished, or only grass fed?

Grass finished. Our cattle never receive grain, at any stage, including the final months. That is the part of the label that changes how the meat eats, and it is the part most producers leave out.

Does it arrive frozen or fresh?

Frozen solid, vacuum sealed, and labelled with the cut, the weight and the date it was packed. Frozen at this stage protects the meat far better than several days in a display case. Thaw in the fridge overnight.

Why is grass-fed beef more expensive?

It takes about eight months longer to bring an animal to weight on grass alone, and it takes considerably more land. You are paying for time and acreage rather than for grain.

If value per pound is what matters most to you, a quarter share brings every cut, ribeyes included, down to a single price. Ring the farm store and we will talk it through.

How should I cook it differently?

Lower and shorter. Grass-fed beef has less intramuscular fat, so it reaches temperature faster and dries out sooner. Take steaks off the heat about 5°F earlier than you would otherwise, and rest them a little longer.

Where is it processed?

At a USDA-inspected facility twenty miles from the farm. The animals travel once, on a short trip, which matters for the animal and for the meat.

Can I pick up instead of paying for delivery?

Yes. Choose collection when you order in our online store and it will be waiting at the farm store on Wagner Road. We are open seven days a week, until 7pm Monday to Saturday and 5pm on Sunday.

Which towns do you deliver to?

Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti, Saline, Dexter and Chelsea. Order by Wednesday at noon and it arrives on Friday. Delivery areas and cut-offs.

How long will it keep in the freezer?

Vacuum-sealed steaks and roasts hold their quality for about a year. Ground beef is best inside six months. Neither becomes unsafe after that, but the texture starts to go.

About our grass-fed beef

Vestergaard Farms sits on 112 acres on Wagner Road, about fifteen minutes from downtown Ann Arbor. The cattle you see in these photographs are the cattle in these packages. They are born here, they graze here, and they stay here until they are ready, somewhere between twenty-six and thirty months.

The herd moves to fresh grass regularly rather than standing on the same ground all season. That pattern gives the pasture time to recover, builds the soil back up, and keeps the animals eating the diet they evolved to eat. No synthetic herbicide, pesticide or fertiliser has ever touched this land, and no genetically modified grain goes into any animal we raise.

What that means for the meat

Grass-finished beef is leaner than grain-finished beef, with a firmer texture and a deeper, more mineral flavour. The fat is a shade more yellow, because of the carotene in fresh grass, which is a sign the animal ate what it should have. It also carries a better ratio of omega-3 to omega-6 fats and more conjugated linoleic acid, though we would rather you bought it because it tastes like something.

Every carcass is dry aged fourteen days on the bone before it is cut. Ageing costs weight, which is why most supermarket beef has had none of it.
