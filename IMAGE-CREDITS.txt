Replace all twenty stock images with photography from the farm. The
shot list is exactly the set above:

  From the homepage    a wide hero frame of the pasture, lamb, salmon,
                       the Michigan-made shelves, a packed bundle box
                       open, and three cooked dishes for the Test
                       Kitchen

                       shot square, plus a packed share and a freezer
                       filled with your own labelled packages

That is one photography day, possibly two if the cuts are shot
properly on a set. Doing it also removes every attribution and
share-alike obligation from the site.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
A NOTE ON THE PRICES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Every price, weight and per-pound figure across all three pages is a
placeholder. The numbers are realistic for pasture-raised beef sold
direct in southeast Michigan, and they are internally consistent with
one another, but they are not the client's. Both beef pages say so on
the page itself, in a design note and in the footer.
